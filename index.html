The problem

AI isn't the strategy. A better business is.

Many SMEs experiment with AI before addressing the underlying problems in their processes, data, accountability and systems.

  • Disconnected technology

    Software that doesn't talk to each other, so information is re-entered and hard to see in one place.

  • Manual administration

    Capable people spending their time on tasks that should run themselves.

  • Information trapped in people's heads

    Important knowledge held by individuals rather than documented in systems.

  • Duplicated work

    The same task handled in more than one place, or by more than one person.

  • Poor management visibility

    Limited reporting, so issues are hard to spot before they matter.

  • Owners becoming the bottleneck

    Decisions and delivery that depend too heavily on the owner.

AI layered over inefficient processes can simply make poor processes happen faster.

Managed Right Intelligence takes a business-first approach to identifying problems and designing appropriate improvements.

The Freedom Equation™

Freedom is multiplicative

Freedom= Clarity× Capability× Consistency× Cash Flow× Time

The concept is deliberately multiplicative.

A business with excellent systems but no cash flow is not free.

A profitable business completely dependent upon its owner is not free.

A well-funded business without strategic clarity is not free.

The objective is to improve the entire equation.

The Freedom Equation™ is a developing proprietary philosophy. It is not a scientifically validated formula, a financial forecasting model or a guaranteed predictor of business outcomes.
